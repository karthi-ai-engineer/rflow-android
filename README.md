# Rflow for Android

**Work in any language, on your phone.** Understand the people around you as they speak, and get your meetings
written up.

Rflow for Android is the phone companion of [Rflow for Windows](https://github.com/karthi-ai-engineer/rflow-ai). It is in
planning: how it will work is in [`docs/plan.md`](docs/plan.md).

## What it will do

**First:**
- **Live translation:** Japanese spoken in the room, or played by a video, read in English as it's said, and heard in
  English too if you like.
- **Meeting mode:** the phone on the table translates live, records, and writes the minutes when the meeting ends.
- **Conversation:** two people, two languages, one phone.

**Later:** the Rflow Keyboard (voice typing in any app), translating and rewriting any text, and the Screen translator.

You bring your own Google Gemini key, as on Windows. It stays on the phone (Android Keystore).
