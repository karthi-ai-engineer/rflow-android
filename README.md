# Rflow for Android

**Work in any language, on your phone.** Voice typing in every app, translation of any text or screen, and
conversations translated as people speak, read aloud if you like.

Rflow for Android is the phone companion of [Rflow for Windows](https://github.com/karthi-ai-engineer/rflow-ai). It is in
planning: how it will work is in [`docs/plan.md`](docs/plan.md).

## What it will do

- **Rflow Keyboard:** hold the mic and speak; clean text is typed into any app.
- **Translate and rewrite:** select text in any app, then Rflow → Translate, Concise, Japanese Teams message...
- **Conversation:** two people, two languages, one phone: each hears and reads the other in their own language.
- **Screen translator:** a Japanese app or screen, translated in place.
- **Captions for videos:** what the phone plays, translated live above any app.
- **Meetings in person:** recorded on the phone, then the minutes written.

You bring your own Google Gemini key, as on Windows. It stays on the phone (Android Keystore).
